5. Finance Knowledge

Essential investment fundamentals, financial concepts, and quantitative valuation metrics.

Investment Basics

Shares (Equities)

Fractional ownership in a public enterprise conferring voting rights and dividends. Higher volatility with long-term compounding potential.

Bonds (Fixed Income)

Debt securities where investors lend money to corporations or governments in exchange for periodic interest and principal return.

Fixed Deposits (FDs)

Guaranteed bank investment offering guaranteed interest over a fixed tenor. Insured up to ₹5 Lakh per depositor by DICGC.

Core Financial Concepts

Compounding

A = P(1 + r/n)^(nt)

Earning returns upon previously accumulated returns, generating geometric exponential curve expansion over prolonged horizons.

Inflation

Purchasing Power Decay

The persistent rise in general price levels over time, requiring investments to achieve higher post-tax yields to preserve real wealth.

Diversification

Unsystematic Risk Reduction

Allocating capital across uncorrelated asset classes so negative volatility in one sector does not compromise overall portfolio stability.
